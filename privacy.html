SpeedBike Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

What we store

  • Account: your email address, username and password. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see or store them in readable form.
  • Game data: your progress (coins, unlocked items, achievements, stats), multiplayer rank and friends list.
  • Security data: when you request a password reset, a scrambled (hashed) form of your IP address is kept for at most 3 minutes so the link only works on the same network. Ban records are kept for moderation.
  • If you play without an account, your progress stays only in your browser.

How we use it

Only to run the game: signing you in, saving your progress, matchmaking, friends and parties. We use your email only for account messages: sign-up verification codes, password reset links and username reminders. No newsletters or advertising.

Who we share it with

We never sell your data. It is processed only by the services that run the game: Google Firebase (accounts and storage), Gmail (sending account emails), Render (game server) and GitHub Pages (website hosting).

Your choices

You can change your username or password, or delete your account and its data, from the account screen in the game. For questions, contact noreply.speedbike@gmail.com.
